# Changelog

## 1.0.6
Developer: Traumlewis

- Removed the ON and SYSTEM text labels from the light control.
- Kept the lightbulb status icon and moved the Down arrow next to it for a compact appearance.
- Light modes, controls and saved preferences remain unchanged.

## 1.0.5
Developer: Traumlewis

- Added a lightbulb control in the bottom-left corner of the start and pause screens.
- Press Down in either menu to switch between ON and SYSTEM.
- ON keeps the backlight on during active gameplay. Pausing, game over, losing focus or closing the app releases the backlight override.
- SYSTEM uses the watch's normal backlight behavior.
- The selected mode is saved across game resets and app restarts.
- Down retains its turning function during gameplay.

## 1.0.4
Developer: Traumlewis

- Added spacing between the screen edges and the Score/High displays.
- Brought the centered clock header to all seven Pebble platforms.
- Kept the additional header divider exclusive to rectangular displays; round models retain their existing playing field frame.
- Added compact header formatting for smaller screens.
- The clock follows the watch's 12/24-hour setting and updates every minute, including while paused and on the start screen.

## 1.0.3
Developer: Traumlewis

- Fixed unused space at the bottom and right edges of the playing field across all Pebble platforms.
- Grid cells now fill the entire playing field, including remainder pixels.
- Pebble Time 2 only: moved Score to the left and High to the right, with the current watch time centered between them.
- Added a horizontal divider below the Time 2 header.
- The clock follows the watch's 12/24-hour setting and updates every minute, including while paused and on the start screen.
- Verified gameplay at the playing field boundaries in all seven platform emulators.
